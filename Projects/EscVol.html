Main Areas of the Project

The main activity was on the platform, with weekly research and reporting being an ongoing activity. Although Social Business has gained relevance in our time, it has not yet been fully embraced by many. Many still struggle to fully grasp the difference between a simple business and a Social Business, or between charity and social business. Another area fraught with questions is the legal aspects, as the laws concerning social businesses differ from those for conventional business models, and this varies from country to country. The mobilisation of financial resources is, at present, the biggest challenge for social businesses. The YesHub platform is addressing all these gaps, implementing activities to resolve the problematic points wherever possible. At the same time, we work on awareness initiatives related to the circular economy. In this area, the focus is not only to explain the theoretical framework of the concept, but to connect it with daily life and the local context. One of the main goals is to encourage people to think more confidently about their decisions related to consumption, entrepreneurship, and idea development. Overall, our activities are not focused on passive information sharing, but on shaping thinking patterns and supporting small but meaningful behavioral changes. For me, this process is especially interesting because it creates an opportunity to combine social engagement with economic logic.

My Role and Contribution

I take part in planning and implementing workshops and training sessions. During the monthly sessions, I work both in organizational tasks and as a facilitator. Currently, my main focus is on a circular economy project that I am developing in its early stage. In this phase, I am working on:

  • Building the structure of the project
  • Identifying stakeholders within the local community
  • Designing the awareness model
  • Testing workshop prototypes

I am working on the conceptual phase of this initiative. The main objective of this initiative is to connect economic and business thinking. By transforming the concept of social business into a practical and global relevant format, I aim to contribute, even in a small way, to a change in thinking at the ommunity level.

Lefkada

Lefkada island is located in the Ionian Sea in Greece. It is one of the few Ionian islands connected to the mainland by a bridge. The island is known for famous beaches such as Porto Katsiki and Egremni, its high cliffs, and its turquoise sea. Lefkada Town is the administrative and cultural center of the island. Areas such as Nydri and Vasiliki are active regions for tourism and water sports.
